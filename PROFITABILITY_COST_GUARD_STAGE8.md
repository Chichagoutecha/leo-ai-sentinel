# Stage 8 — Profitability & Cost Guard

Status: **SHADOW ANALYTICS ONLY**.

Attributes gross P&L, spreads, slippage, fees, OpenAI cost, data cost and infrastructure cost to decisions, sources and routing modes. Reports net P&L, win rate, cost drag, ROI-on-cost and AI budget consumption.

The module does not call OpenAI, trade, block LIVE or change budgets. Acceptance requires reconciled cost inputs and sufficient matched outcome samples before conclusions are used. Rollback is removal of the module import/preload.
