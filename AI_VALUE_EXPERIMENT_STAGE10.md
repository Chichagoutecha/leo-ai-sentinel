# Stage 10 — AI Value Experiment

Status: **SHADOW EVALUATION ONLY**.

Compares `QUANT_ONLY`, `QUANT_AI` and `COUNCIL` on the **same opportunity IDs**. Unmatched records are excluded from the matched comparison. Reports return, hit rate, cost-adjusted net return and incremental AI cost.

No value verdict is issued before the minimum matched sample. The module performs no model calls and cannot promote an arm to LIVE automatically.

Acceptance requires sufficient out-of-sample matched observations and stable cost-adjusted advantage. Rollback is removal of the experiment module.
